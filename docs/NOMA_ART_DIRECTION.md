# Noma — Locked Genesis Art Direction

Noma is the second production-ready Arc Companion family. This document locks the Genesis identity so future assets and evolution forms stay consistent.

## Core identity

- Young, floating spirit-creature rather than a humanoid mascot.
- Calm, observant, empathetic and slightly mysterious.
- Distinct from Vexa: no fox silhouette, no pointed ears, no quadruped anatomy.
- Signature anatomy: luminous axolotl-like side fins, large reflective eyes, a living chest memory core, and a flowing spirit tail/body.
- The silhouette must remain readable at small card and avatar sizes.

## Palette

- Pearl body base.
- Lilac and soft violet as primary energy tones.
- Icy blue and mint as secondary bioluminescent accents.
- Arc lime/pale gold is reserved for signal/core highlights rather than dominating the full body.

## Production rules

- The Genesis form should feel young and capable of growth, not Ascended or final-form powerful.
- Avoid generic alien, fairy, blob or human-torso anatomy.
- Keep surface detail organic and luminous rather than metallic.
- Preserve the forehead signal mark, side-fin anatomy, eye shape, memory core and flowing tail across all Noma assets.
- App UI text, wallet data and archetype labels must remain separate from the character artwork.
- Archetype changes personality presentation only; it must not redraw Noma into another species.

## Evolution seeds

Noma has two permanent paths:

- **Nymora** — ethereal, elegant, intuitive, flowing.
- **Noryx** — psionic, focused, powerful, more assertive.

Ascended targets:

- **Nymoria** — Ascended Nymora path.
- **Noryth** — Ascended Noryx path.

Every evolution stage must introduce at least three major morphological changes. Do not create the impression that the same Genesis character was merely enlarged or given extra ornaments.

The Genesis design intentionally leaves room for future changes in fin architecture, core complexity, tail structure, head crest, body proportion and energy anatomy.

## Product usage

The canonical in-app Genesis hero is served as `/assets/noma-genesis.webp`.

Recommended derivative assets:

- Hero/reveal image: dark cosmic background, no embedded account-specific UI text.
- Transparent character master: reusable for cards, share images and future scenes.
- Avatar crop: face + forehead signal + upper memory core.
- Concept/lore poster: external storytelling and documentation only, not rendered as live app UI.

Noma Genesis is considered visually locked. Future work should extend this identity rather than redesign it.
